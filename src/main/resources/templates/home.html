Good to know

Common questions.

A few things worth knowing before you start.

Does SkillSwap cost money?

No. Students exchange knowledge and time. Skill Credits are in-app rewards only — not money, not transferable out, and not withdrawable.

Do I need to be an expert to teach?

No. Be clear about your experience and choose a skill you feel comfortable sharing. Learning goes both ways.

How are matches chosen?

Recommendations use real skill overlap, reciprocal interests, availability, proficiency, and learning preferences from student profiles.

Can I choose who sees my profile?

Yes. Profile visibility and what information you share are controlled in your account settings.
